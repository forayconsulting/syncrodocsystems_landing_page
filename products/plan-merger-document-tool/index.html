Who it’s for

  • Multiemployer medical and pension funds in a merger
  • Plan sponsors
  • ERISA counsel
  • Benefits consultants

When two or more medical or pension plans have to become one, and someone has to know exactly what changes for whom.

The problem

The same benefit, written a different way in every plan.

  • 01Each plan describes the same benefit in different words, in different places.
  • 02Side-by-side comparison by hand takes weeks of attorney and staff time.
  • 03A difference missed in the merger becomes a promise the merged plan never meant to make.

What it does

The whole document set, compared by meaning.

01

Compares the whole set

SPDs, plan documents and amendments for every plan in the merger.

02

Matches provisions by meaning

Eligibility, vesting, benefit formulas and appeal rules line up even when the wording differs.

03

Marks every difference

Each provision is marked modified, added or removed, plan by plan.

04

Cites every line

Each difference links to the page and language in each plan.

What you get

Outcomes your team and your board can see.

Weeks back

The comparison runs in minutes, so counsel spends its time on decisions.

Nothing missed

Every provision in every plan is compared, not a sample.

A clear decision record

Trustees see exactly what they are choosing between.
