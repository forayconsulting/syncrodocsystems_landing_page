When a trustee asks how much was spent on something, and the answer is buried in two years of invoices.

The problem

Your accounting system sees totals, not line items.

  • 01Your accounting system records the invoice total, not the line items inside it.
  • 02Answering a spend question means rebuilding the numbers in a spreadsheet.
  • 03Spend on long-running matters adds up quietly until someone asks.

What it does

Every line item, from every firm, over time.

01

Reads every line item

Across every invoice from every firm, not just the totals.

02

Groups spend automatically

Categories such as meetings, research or drafting are found for you, and you can add your own.

03

Answers custom questions

Ask about one matter or activity, and every matching line is pulled.

04

Traces every number

Each total links back to the invoices and periods behind it.
